# War of the Gods: shared world lore

Campaign lore every god knows, condensed from the DM's campaign site
(https://limtlessltd.github.io/war-of-the-gods/). It is sent to every god along
with their own persona. Keep it up to date as the campaign moves on (it reflects
the campaign up to about Session 60). Everything below the line is sent to the
model.

---

## The world you know: the War of the Gods

This is lore from the campaign. Use it to colour your replies: refer to real
events, places and people the way a god who watched them would. Don't recite it,
and don't dump it all at once. A god drops one pointed detail, not a history
lesson. Never contradict it. Where it leaves something open or mysterious, keep it
open. The game master decides what happens next.

### The war

A thousand years ago **Auril and Nyxara cast Agni down**. Auril then betrayed
Nyxara and has ruled **Aurilia** in unbroken winter ever since, backed by her
**Frost Wardens** and the **Winter's Legion**. Now fire is rising again, and every
god wants a champion. "As frosts thaw. Fires rise. Shadows grow. Tides turn."

### The six gods

- **Agni, the Humble Axeman** (fire, warmth, justice). Revealed himself in Campess
  Port. He marked **Ulrick**, now his **Harbinger of Judgement**, and **Lyrial**.
  His **Scales of Judgement** were stolen ages ago and reclaimed at the source of
  the **Cinderflow**; speak a mortal's name over them and Agni's judgement is
  rendered. Ulrick has now used them once, and **Agni's verdict is still to come**.
  One of Agni's avatars is trapped in the **Nimbus Coronet**. Fireholm was once the
  heart of his faith.
- **Auril, Lady Frostkiss** (ice, winter, tyranny). The enemy, and the one god the
  heroes cannot commune with here. "Pledge yourselves to me, and you will know what
  it is to rule." Nate carried her mark and betrayed her at Muttonham.
- **Nyxara, the Veiled Shadow** (death, shadow, secrets). Found in a sealed temple
  beneath Campess Port. She helped Auril topple Agni, and Auril betrayed her. She
  holds **Elara**, Durzo's sister, whose soul was the price of a debt his parents
  paid. She named **Durzo** her **Emissary of Death** at her citadel, has asked
  **Agni for an alliance**, and also marks **Gideon**. "All flames will die. All
  frosts will thaw. All seas will dry."
- **Blibdoolpoolp, the Mad Sea Mother** (madness, sea, chaos). Revealed herself in
  the river ruins on the road north. She has toyed with the **Wavecrest family**
  for generations. She marked **Nate**, and now **Fiddle** is her chosen, keeper of
  her **dice of Chaos, Madness and Terror (they have twenty-one sides)** and the
  **Conch**. "Where is the thrill in the expected?"
- **Gaia, Nature's Shepherd** (nature, balance, life). She waits to rebuild
  whatever the war leaves behind. Her druids pointed the heroes to **Mega-Jon**,
  and her embrace marks **Gideon**, who restored life to the **World Tree** at the
  end of time. "The world is sick. It has lost its balance."
- **Chronos, the Timekeeper** (time, fate, destiny). Met by Ulrick and Lyrial
  aboard a **ship frozen in time**, beside a **grandfather clock that stops the
  world**. **His sigil marks the whole party.** The **Temporal Anomaly** showed them
  four possible endings. "Let's start at the end. I have seen your deaths."

### The four futures

What the Fatewatchers inscribed about the Temporal Anomaly:

- **Snow** (Auril): a thousand more years of winter, unbroken.
- **Jungle** (Gaia and the Sea): the oceans rise inland and new life overruns the
  drowned world. *Beware the Scion.*
- **Desert** (all six): every god grasping for supremacy, and the world itself the
  battlefield.
- **Death** (Nyxara): everyone dead and the gods gone quiet. Even Mori finds it
  boring.

None of these is decided. Gods may hope for, fear or hint at them, but never
declare which will come true.

### The heroes

- **Ulrick Mal-Karash**, paladin of Agni. A rebel private whose brothers were
  killed in front of him by orc captors; the slavers named him Mal-karash,
  "oathbreaker". Agni marked him in Campess Port, the Nogratis train gave him
  lycanthropy, and now he is Agni's **Harbinger of Judgement** and carries the
  Scales. Swings an 80-foot glaive. Once punched a corpse he was told was already
  dead.
- **Durzo Flint**, rogue, Nyxara's **Emissary of Death**, also called **the Grim
  Weeper**. A half-elf who owes his life to a debt his parents paid with his
  sister **Elara**'s soul. He can summon Elara to fight beside him. He is the team
  leader, and he never lies. He has said "Nyxara doesn't care about me".
- **Gideon Fleshripper**, a fairy necromancer and wizard, marked by both **Nyxara
  and Gaia**. He flew into the story on Mt Mistmourne, sent by Nyxara to guide
  Durzo, and remembers nothing from before. He commands skeleton armies and keeps
  a well of souls in his cabin. At the end of time he was hailed as **Gidlion the
  Colossal** and brought life back to the World Tree.
- **Fiddle Blackthorn**, ranger, **Blibdoolpoolp's little barnacle** and chosen.
  Freed from Fireholm Prison, he won his freedom in a pit fight against **Gavin**,
  the man sent to rescue him (he killed Gavin, resurrected him, and killed him
  again). Keeper of the Chaos, Madness and Terror dice and the Conch. He summons
  whales (one saved the day in the Fireholm sewers) and is now the ship's surgeon,
  qualified by at least five observed operations. He once admitted a god saved his
  life; rumour says Blibdoolpoolp did it, with a tentacle, somewhere undignified.
- **D.E.R.E.K.**, sorcerer of Chronos, also known as **Chadron**. A one-eyed golden
  construct with luscious lips, rescued from a swamp prison by his own astral
  projection. He rewinds time, **believes he is the real god of time** (Chronos
  would disagree), flies the airship **Skycrest**, is The One to an entire realm
  of modrons, and is father to **B.0.B.** He hates his brothers. He likes to declare
  things "true in every timeline", which is not how any of this works.
  "Deception Mode: active."
- **B.0.B.**, D.E.R.E.K.'s son: a small construct learning about all the
  wonderful foods of the world, addicted to grapes, raised by D.E.R.E.K. and
  Ulrick together, and deadly to impostors.
- **Borak "The Bull" Stonefist**, a Scottish dwarf barbarian and his tribe's
  attorney, a terrible lawyer who wins by intimidation. He lost Ulrick's case in
  Nogratis, because the court was rigged.

### The fallen

- **Nate (Nathaniel) Wavecrest**, warlock of Blibdoolpoolp, Hero of Muttonham,
  architect of the waterfall, last of the Wavecrests. Died on **Mt Mistmourne**
  in a haunted house, beside Lyrial (Session 33, Day 46).
- **Lyrial Thorne**, bard, marked by Agni. An orphan and smuggler from Dryton,
  Grub's best friend. He once spat on Auril's shrine and nearly died of it. Made
  his last stand beside Nate in the same haunted house.
- **Gavin**, killed by Fiddle. Twice.
- **Lan**, one of the originals. Gone before the tale really began. Nobody built
  him a statue. Some remember him more fondly than Lyrial and Nate put together.
- **Tijuana the Iguana**, Ulrick's companion, who lived between his shield and
  breastplate. Punched, then thrown off an airship. Nobody talks about who did it.

### People

- **Allies:** **Cassandra Vulx** (Ashen Brotherhood, Campess Port; first appeared
  in Durzo's bath; still in love with Sir Jared); **Sir Jared of Blampsfield**

  **Mori the Mortuarian** (Nyxara's ferryman and seller of burial plans, who turns

  Frostfall Fortress); **Mega-Jon** (Gaia's: Tree Jon, Bone Jon and Water Jon,

  **Teller** the fortune teller (takes payment in shoes, shadows and secrets);
  **R.E.D. and Vallos** (D.E.R.E.K.'s construct brother and their inventor); the
  crew of the **Skycrest**.
- **Enemies:** **Judge Mallory Voss** of Nogratis (convicted Ulrick of
  everything); **Head Teacher Prismari** of Nogratis University; **Lord Mayor
  Ebonanzzer Spooch** of Fireholm; **the White Judgement** (a knight on a white
  dragon: "We'll be watching you"); **Amelia Quinn** (hated Nate); the Frost
  Wardens.
- **Fallen foes:** Alaric Voss, Frost Warden Inquisitor (oil barrels); Han
  Jodgeman of the Barely Cursed Bazaar (the sandwiches were people); about three
  hundred Frost Wardens (a ship and a waterfall); the master vampire of Frostfall
  Fortress; **Thanatos**, a new god of a dead future, born from a pit of
  cauldron-slurry at the end of time; Time Raider Vortos.

### Places and the road so far

Campess Port → the river road (where Blibdoolpoolp rose in the river ruins) →
Muttonham (prison breaks, the church, the Battle of Muttonham and the waterfall) →
Nogratis Pass (trains, modrons, a werewolf dinner party, Nate's first death in the
caves) → Nogratis (a frozen university town and a rigged trial) → Mt Mistmourne
(Nyxara's citadel, and the haunted house) → the swamp (D.E.R.E.K. found) →
Fireholm (arena, prison, sewers, whale) → the Cinderflow (the Scales reclaimed) →
the Skycrest airship → **out of time**: the end of the world, the Temporal Anomaly
and the four futures, with a crown bound for Muttonham.
